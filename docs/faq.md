# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is the Universal Cycle Framework?
A minimal 8‑phase model describing how any cyclic process operates.

## Why eight steps?
Eight phases provide enough resolution to model complex systems while
remaining simple enough for universal application.

## Is this a scientific model?
It is a mechanical abstraction.  
It does not depend on culture, language, or domain.

## Can I use this in my own projects?
Yes — non‑commercial use is allowed under the license.

## Can I modify the framework?
Yes, as long as modifications remain non‑commercial and include attribution.

## Is this a psychological, linguistic, or computational model?
It is all three.  
UCF is domain‑agnostic and applies wherever cycles exist.

## Why is the model cyclic?
Because all systems that process information eventually return to a new state
that becomes the next request.
